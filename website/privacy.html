Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

BK BOT processes the minimal data required to provide its services. When you authorize the bot, we store your Discord user ID, username and avatar for authentication. When a server adds the bot, we store server settings, custom commands, level data, warnings and backups that the server administrators create.

2. How We Use Data

Data is used exclusively to operate the bot and dashboard: applying your configuration, generating backups, displaying server statistics and moderating your server according to your settings. We do not sell, rent or share your data with any third party.

3. Dashboard Security

The dashboard is accessible only to server administrators through Discord OAuth2. Sessions use encrypted, rotating tokens with a limited lifetime. Backups created through the dashboard are visible only to administrators of the guild that created them.

4. Data Retention

Server data (settings, levels, warnings, backups) is retained until removed by the server administrators through the dashboard or until the bot is removed from the server. Session data expires automatically.

5. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your data at any time by removing the bot from your server or contacting us. Removal from a server deletes that server's stored configuration and backups.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Baktiriya Team through the support links on our website.
